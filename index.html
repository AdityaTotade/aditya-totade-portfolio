Turning foremen’s messages into checked daily reports

Proposal & architecture

The problem
Foremen send unstructured daily updates by email, WhatsApp and text.
The design
  • A language model reads the messages and pulls out what was said. That is its only job.
  • Fixed rules handle matching, duplicates, arithmetic and required fields.
  • A person approves anything billable, such as potential change orders.
The principle
Each part does only what it is reliable at, and a person keeps the final say.

How a project with me runs, step by step.

Most system problems I see turn out to be process problems. So I start with how the work happens day to day, and nothing counts as done until the people who use it have tested it.

  1. A free Workflow Audit call Free

    We talk for 15–20 minutes about how the process runs, including the parts nobody wrote down. I won’t try to sell you anything on the call.

  2. A written proposal

    If it’s a fit, I send a scoped proposal: the problem, the current state and the desired state, then the deliverables, timeline and price. You know exactly what you’re getting before anything starts.

  3. Fixed-timeline delivery

    I work to the agreed timeline, and the people who will use the system test it (user acceptance testing, or UAT) before we sign it off.

  4. Handover you can own

    You get documentation and a walkthrough, so your team can run it without me. I’ll also tell you what I finished, what I tested and what I left out.

  • Diagnose before building
  • Validate with real users
  • No black boxes
  • A person approves what matters
  • Be clear about what’s finished and what isn’t
